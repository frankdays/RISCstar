  • Integrating new silicon and custom board designs quickly and reliably
  • Meeting strict real-time and power constraints across diverse hardware
  • Ensuring product and data security across the full device lifecycle
  • Supporting wireless connectivity standards
  • Controlling long-term maintenance costs and technical debt

Without the right expertise, integration delays and software instability can slow time-to-market and erode product margins. RISCstar provides the Zephyr engineering depth to overcome these challenges and deploy with confidence.

RISCstar's Approach to Zephyr Engineering

RISCstar engages from concept through deployment to ensure your product is ready for market success:

Board Bring-Up & Hardware Enablement

We bring up new silicon quickly — including clocks, power domains, and memory initialization — and deliver production-quality BSPs for your custom hardware designs.

Device Driver Development

We deliver high-performance driver support for sensors, connectivity modules, displays, and peripherals, aligned with Zephyr’s driver model and APIs.

Connectivity Integration

We implement end-to-end wireless stacks including BLE, Wi-Fi, 802.15.4, Thread, and Matter — fully integrated and validated on your target hardware.

Real-Time & Power Optimization

We analyze, profile, and tune your system to meet strict timing and power budgets, ensuring reliable real-time behavior across your full range of operating conditions.

Security & OTA Update Integration

We implement secure boot using MCUboot, build firmware-update pipelines, and deliver lifecycle management that keeps your devices secure in the field.

Upstream Contribution & Long-Term Maintenance

We align your product with Zephyr mainline, contribute patches upstream, and automate CI workflows — reducing technical debt and long-term maintenance overhead.
